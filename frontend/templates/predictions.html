How scores and risk levels work

The classifier predicts the probability that a learner will have low / medium / high engagement in the next 14 days, using the previous 28 days of activity and recent assessments.

score = 100 × (0.5 × P(medium) + 1.0 × P(high))

  • High risk score < {{ high_below|num(0) }}
  • Medium risk {{ high_below|num(0) }} ≤ score < {{ medium_below|num(0) }}
  • Low risk score ≥ {{ medium_below|num(0) }}

Thresholds are configurable in .env. Predictions are decision-support estimates only.
